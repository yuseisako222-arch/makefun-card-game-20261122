Ember & Ivy — a solo card duel at the Court of the Two Orders. As the Ember Mage you face the Ivy Warden across a felt table: nine kinds of cards (damage, healing, shields, venom, mirrors and gusts), 3 energy a turn, 30 life each, and an AI opponent that heals when it is losing and strikes when it is not. Static files, no build step, no network: open it and play with mouse or keyboard.
