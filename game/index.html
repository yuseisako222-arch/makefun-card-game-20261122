Solo card duel

Ember&Ivy

Ember faces the Ivy Warden for the overgrown court’s laurels.

1–7 Cast a card ◆ Space End turn

How to Play

Take your turn. Refill to 3 energy, take any poison damage, then draw 2. Extra draws beyond 7 cards go face-up to the discard.

Cast your spells. Click a card or press 1–7 to cast it. End Turn or Space passes to Ivy. First to 0 life loses.

Read the court. Healing caps at 30. Shield takes priority over Mirror. Reapplying Venom resets it to 3 ticks. Empty decks reshuffle their discard.
